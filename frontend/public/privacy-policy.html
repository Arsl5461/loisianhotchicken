Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how LHC (Louisiana Hot Chicken) (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use our website, web dashboard, and mobile applications for Android and iOS (together, the “Services”).

LHC is an internal business operations tool for authorized Louisiana Hot Chicken team members. It is not a consumer ordering app and is not directed to children.

2. Who this policy covers

  • Authorized users of the LHC web admin dashboard
  • Authorized users of the LHC Android and iOS apps
  • Visitors who open this privacy policy page

3. Information we collect

Account and profile information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Password (stored only in hashed form)
  • Role, permissions, and assigned stores
  • Optional organization profile details such as phone number and address

Authentication and security data

  • Sign-in credentials and one-time verification codes sent by email
  • Access and refresh tokens used to keep you signed in
  • Password reset codes
  • Basic login activity needed to protect accounts

Business and operational data you enter

  • Store information
  • Sales and sale categories
  • Expenses, receipts, and payment methods
  • Borrowings and repayments
  • Reports and related financial summaries

Device and technical information

  • IP address and approximate request metadata
  • Browser or app type, device type, and operating system
  • App version and basic diagnostic/error logs
  • Network connection details required to communicate with our servers

Media you choose to upload

  • Photos, images, or files attached as expense receipts or sales attachments
  • Camera or photo-library access is used only when you choose to attach a file

4. How we use information

  • Authenticate users and manage secure sessions
  • Provide multi-store operations features such as sales, expenses, borrowings, and reporting
  • Enforce role-based access and store-level permissions
  • Send sign-in and password-reset codes by email
  • Maintain security, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot issues
  • Improve reliability and support of the Services
  • Comply with legal obligations that apply to our business

We do not use your data for advertising or personalized ads.

5. How we share information

We do not sell personal information.

We may share information only with:

  • Service providers that help us host, store, secure, or operate the Services (for example cloud hosting, email delivery, and infrastructure providers), under agreements that limit their use of the data
  • Authorized organization administrators within Louisiana Hot Chicken who manage users, stores, and operations data
  • Legal authorities when required by law, court order, or to protect rights, safety, and security

6. Data storage and security

We store account and operational data on our servers and related infrastructure. We use industry standard protections such as HTTPS encryption in transit, hashed passwords, authenticated API access, and role-based permissions.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We work to protect your information, but we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data retention

We retain account and business records for as long as needed to operate the Services, meet business and accounting needs, resolve disputes, and comply with legal requirements. When an account is deactivated or deletion is requested, we will remove or anonymize personal account data where reasonably possible, subject to records we must keep for legitimate business or legal reasons.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Access or update profile and organization information in Settings
  • Request that an administrator update or deactivate your account
  • Request deletion of personal account data by contacting us
  • Sign out to clear local session tokens on your device

To request access, correction, or deletion, email admin@louisianahotchicken.com.

9. Children’s privacy

The Services are intended for authorized adult business users. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

10. International access

The Services may be accessed from different locations. If you use the Services from outside the country where our servers are located, your information may be processed in jurisdictions with different data-protection laws.

11. Third-party links and services

The Services may interact with infrastructure providers needed to run hosting, email, and file storage. Those providers process data only as needed to deliver their services to us. This policy does not cover unrelated third-party websites you may visit outside the Services.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date on this page. Continued use of the Services after changes means you acknowledge the updated policy.
